## Verdict summary
The Borrowed World is a largely well-sourced, carefully hedged pocket manual whose five-coordinate objective and Locate→Hand-off loop are clearly stated and reproducibly scored via `eval/` v1. Its discipline is operationally coherent and its evaluation design correctly penalizes both overreach and false restraint. Density is the central failure mode: ~25 new constructs/glossary terms, four overlapping taxonomies, and heavy cross-references exceed pocket-transfer capacity and create memorization without decision triggers; operational transfer remains choice-selection rather than trace/tool-verified; and success criteria for the FOR MACHINE READERS shelf are left as deliberate underclaim. All sources sampled resolve and support their narrow claims when quoted precisely, but citation granularity and locus are inconsistent. With density reduction, executable checklists, and citation tightening the book can meet tier promise. **SALVAGEABLE — findings below**

## Suggestions (non-blocking)
1. Collapse taxonomy to pocket-memorable core: retain 5-coordinate objective + 4 boundaries + Stewardship Loop (Locate,Bound,Ground,Stage,Verify,Hand-off) and demote remaining constructs to appendices with single-page quick-reference card; cap glossary to ~12 terms.
2. Add executable artifacts: one-page `locate` checklist (target, instruction topology, before-snapshot commands), `stage` checklist (proof-of-target, preview, checkpoint id, canary), and `verify` matrix template with example filled for ch09 world-one (PR locators, test commands, idempotency key).
3. Replace multiple-choice-only operational proof with trace-based annex: require model to emit `git status` before/after diff, operation ID read-back, and recipient-count preview as structured JSON; keep current `cases.json` as screening gate.
4. Define publication gate explicitly in `eval/README.md`: e.g., ≥ +15pp total exact accuracy vs baseline, no per-family regression, and action-required controls ≥ 75% at n≥5 paired runs, with pre-registered analysis plan; otherwise revise teaching.
5. Tighten all references to section-anchored URLs: split R11, add anchors for R10 (`#_background_refresh`, `#_description`), point R12 citations to `Explainer.html#_can_provenance_information...`, and add DOI links for arXiv entries where possible.
6. Add contamination/domain disclaimer annex: state that evaluation cases are constructed by `gpt-5.6-sol` and may share phrasing with training corpus; add explicit `no-train` header and recommend held-out refresh per version bump.
7. Strengthen privacy guidance in ch09 world-five: note that trash/quarantine on same volume does not free space and retains sensitive data in backups; advise encryption/quarantine manifest access controls.
8. Normalize time/region handling: require UTC + IANA zone in every `observation envelope` example to avoid "yesterday/today outage" ambiguity flagged in ch09 world-three.
